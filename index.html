How to Connect with Friends:

  1. Start a chat: Click "Start New Chat" to generate your unique chat ID
  2. Share your ID: Send your chat ID to your friend (via any messaging app)
  3. Your friend connects: They open Evira and enter your chat ID
  4. Chat securely: Messages delete automatically after selected time
